<h2> About Me </h2>

I'm a Nanotechnology Engineering graduate from the University of Waterloo (Nanoelectronics specialization). I build hands-on tools and materials at the nanoscale, from magnetic nanoparticle sensors to nanocomposites, and I enjoy taking a project from synthesis and experimental design through to a working, validated prototype. My capstone, MAGNADIAN, combined nanomaterial synthesis, sensor calibration, and embedded programming into a real-time benchtop diagnostic tool.

**Skills**
- **Materials Characterization:** SEM, XRD, FTIR, UV-Vis, DSC, COD testing
- **Materials Processing:** Nanocomposite fabrication, nanomaterial synthesis, magnetic nanoparticles, heat treatment
- **Research & Development:** Design of Experiments (DOE), process optimization, prototype validation, technical documentation
- **Electronics & Instrumentation:** TMR sensors, sensor calibration, embedded microcontrollers, oscilloscope, digital multimeter, soldering
- **Software & Analysis:** Python, MATLAB, Origin, Excel, SolidWorks

<h2>:memo: Engineering Projects:</h2>

- [Capstone: MAGNADIAN](https://github.com/rianesguerra07/CapstoneMAGNADIAN) — A real-time benchtop sensor that uses tunneling magnetoresistance (TMR) sensors and functionalized magnetic nanoparticles to detect microplastic contamination in industrial hydrogen peroxide.
